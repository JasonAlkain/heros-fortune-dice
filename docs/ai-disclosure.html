HOW THIS TOOL WAS MADE

AI disclosure & credits

A clear account of AI assistance, runtime behavior, and responsibility for the project.

Updated November 15, 2026

01 / THE SHORT VERSION

AI helped build it. AI does not pick your rolls.

Hero’s Fortune was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI’s ChatGPT/Codex, including generated code, interface work, documentation, and testing support. The project owner directs development and makes edits to the repository.

No AI model runs your dice rolls.

The browser generates random candidate dice, then applies the selected luck level and roll mode. The roller does not call an AI service to choose results, read your character, or decide what would make a better story.

02 / WHAT CHANGES THE ODDS

Luck is an explicit house rule

Fair is the default on a fresh page load. Gentle, Heroic, and Legendary give each die a chance to roll a second candidate and keep the higher result. Advantage or disadvantage is applied after those candidates are compared.

The weighting is a deliberate feature, separate from AI’s role in development. A bad result can still happen. Legendary currently permits natural 20s; a proposed cap of 19 has not been implemented.

Choose your luck level with your table’s agreement. The beginner guide explains the odds and shows worked examples.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Assistance is not a guarantee of correctness

AI-generated and human-written work can contain bugs, unclear wording, or incorrect assumptions. Tests and browser checks help find problems, but they do not prove every device, browser, or edge case works correctly.

Project maintenance and decisions remain with the project owner. AI assistance does not replace responsibility for investigating and fixing reported issues.

If something looks wrong, report it on GitHub. Include the dice settings, expected behavior, and what actually happened. Please do not include private campaign details or personal information.
