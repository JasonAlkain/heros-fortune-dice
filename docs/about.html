About & feedback

Why Hero’s Fortune exists, who maintains it, and how to help improve it.

Updated November 15, 2026

01 / WHY IT EXISTS

A little luck, by agreement

Hero’s Fortune is a free browser-based dice roller for tabletop groups that want quick action setups and a clear view of their odds.

It started with a simple idea: give groups an optional way to favor higher rolls and make their heroes feel stronger. The result keeps the luck setting visible, offers ordinary Fair dice by default, and lets players inspect how each roll was selected.

It works best when the table chooses its house rules together. It is a rolling aid, not a character sheet, virtual tabletop, or full rules engine.

03 / HELP IMPROVE IT

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Check existing issues first to see whether someone has already reported the same problem.

Report a bug ↗Suggest a feature

For a useful bug report, include:

  • What you expected and what actually happened.
  • The steps that reproduce it.
  • Dice count, die type, modifier, mode, and luck setting.
  • Your browser and device; a screenshot if it helps.
GitHub reports are public.

Remove personal information and private campaign details. These links open a draft issue; nothing is submitted until you choose to submit it on GitHub. GitHub may ask you to sign in.
