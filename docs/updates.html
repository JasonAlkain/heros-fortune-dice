02 / RELEASED EARLIER

What is already available

  • Named action rows with individual dice settings, modifiers, and roll modes; roll one action or the whole tray.
  • Fair, Gentle, Heroic, and Legendary luck, with visible odds and candidate-die details.
  • Browser-saved action setups and the most recent 30 rolls.
  • Light/dark themes and remembered theme preferences.
  • A How it works guide with examples of dice notation, luck, modes, and odds.
  • Theme-loading improvements, versioned asset URLs, and readability improvements to the source.

03 / CURRENT LIMITS

What to keep in mind

  • One die type per action row. Different die types require separate rows.
  • Roll all rolls every row independently; it does not resolve attacks, apply damage, or enforce game rules.
  • Modes apply to each die individually, including multi-die actions.
  • Saved data is local to the browser. No cloud sync, export/import, or shared party sessions.
  • History records kept values and totals, not a permanent archive of all candidate dice.
  • Legendary still allows natural 20s. No critical-hit cap has been added.

These are current behavior and feature limits, rather than a claim that every reported bug has been resolved.

04 / IDEAS TO DISCUSS

Under consideration

IDEAS · NOT IMPLEMENTED

  • Export and import: move action setups between browsers and keep backups.
  • Named trays: organize separate setups for characters or encounters.
  • Additional luck tradeoffs: explore optional limits, such as a clearly disclosed cap on natural d20 results.

These are discussion topics, not release commitments. There are no promised dates or guaranteed implementations.
